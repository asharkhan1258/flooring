Laminate Flooring FAQs

What is laminate flooring made of?

Laminate flooring is a multi-layer synthetic product designed to look like wood, stone, or tile. It typically consists of a durable wear layer, a high-resolution photographic layer, a dense core board, and a stabilizing backing layer. This construction makes laminate both attractive and resistant to everyday wear.

Is laminate flooring durable and easy to maintain?

Yes. Laminate flooring is known for its durability. It resists scratches, stains, and fading, making it ideal for high-traffic areas and homes with pets or children. Maintenance is simple: regular sweeping or vacuuming and occasional damp mopping are usually enough to keep it looking great.

Can laminate flooring be installed in any room?

Laminate flooring works well in most rooms, including living rooms, bedrooms, and hallways. However, it’s not recommended for areas with excessive moisture, such as bathrooms or laundry rooms, unless the product is specifically designed to be water-resistant.
