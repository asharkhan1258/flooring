Tile and Backsplash FAQs

How long does tile installation typically take?

Tile installation time varies depending on the size of the area, but most residential projects can be completed within 1-3 days.

Can I install a backsplash myself, or should I hire a professional?

While DIY is possible, hiring a professional like T.A. Roberts Flooring, LLC ensures proper installation and a polished finish.

What types of tile are best for high-traffic areas?

Porcelain and ceramic tiles are excellent choices for high-traffic areas due to their durability and ease of maintenance.
